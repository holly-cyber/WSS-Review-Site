## FAQ

### Is Veloforte Limon suitable for runners as well as cyclists?

Absolutely — the lighter gel texture and natural lemon flavour make it well-suited to running, where GI sensitivity is often heightened. The 45g carb load is ideal for marathon or ultra-distance runners targeting 60–90g of carbohydrate per hour from around the 60-minute mark.

### How many gels do I need per hour?

For a 90g/hour carbohydrate target, you'd need two **Limon** gels per hour — which tracks with current endurance nutrition guidance. For lighter fuelling targets (60g/hour), one gel every 45 minutes works well. Always take with water.

### Does Veloforte Limon contain caffeine?

Based on available product information, the standard **Limon** gel does not contain caffeine. If you rely on caffeine for late-race performance, you may want to supplement with a caffeinated option or check Veloforte's wider range for caffeinated variants.

### Can I use Limon for shorter training sessions?

You can, but it's most effective — and most cost-efficient — for efforts over 90 minutes, when exogenous carbohydrate fuelling genuinely impacts performance. For sessions under an hour, a small snack or sports drink will usually suffice without the expense of a high-carb gel.
